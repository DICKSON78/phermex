1. Who we are

Helix ("we", "us", "our") operates the Helix pharmacy app, a service that connects people with licensed pharmacies in Tanzania so they can order medicines, upload prescriptions and receive deliveries.

We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy.

  • Email: support@helix.co.tz
  • Phone: +255 669 254 444
  • Website: helix.co.tz

2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what we collect when you install and use the Helix app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the controls you have.

The short version: we collect what is needed to deliver your medicines — who you are, where to deliver, what you ordered and how to reach you. We do not sell your personal information. We do not store your card or mobile money credentials. Your payment for medicines goes directly to the pharmacy that fills your order.

5. How we use your information

  • To create, secure and maintain your account.
  • To process orders and pass them to the pharmacy you chose.
  • To arrange delivery, show live tracking and notify you of progress.
  • To let a pharmacist verify a prescription before dispensing.
  • To host booked video consultations.
  • To send you transactional notifications: order confirmations, ready-for-delivery alerts, prescription results and consultation reminders.
  • To operate loyalty rewards you have joined.
  • To provide customer support and investigate anything that goes wrong.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and diagnose technical problems.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations that apply to pharmacies and to us.
  • To understand aggregate, de-identified usage of the app so we can improve it. We do not use your health information for advertising.

We rely on your consent where the law requires it (for example, optional location), on the contract between you and us to deliver your order, and on our legitimate interests to keep the service safe and reliable.

6. Payments

Buying medicines. You pay the pharmacy directly — in cash on delivery, or by sending mobile money to the account that pharmacy publishes in the app. That money never passes through Helix. We record only the payment status the pharmacy sets, so we do not receive your mobile money number, PIN or card details for those purchases.

Pharmacy subscriptions. Pharmacies that subscribe to Helix pay by card or mobile money through our payment provider, ClickPesa. Card numbers and mobile money credentials are entered on the provider's payment page and are never stored on our servers.

Card and mobile money data is therefore handled entirely by the relevant provider under its own terms and privacy policy. We never ask you to send us a PIN by chat, SMS or phone call.

8. Location data

Location access is optional. We ask for it only so we can show pharmacies near you, place you on the map, and — if you switch on live tracking — show where your delivery is.

  • If you deny location, you can still place orders by typing your delivery address.
  • You can turn location off at any time in your device settings; live tracking then stops.
  • We do not track your location when the app is closed, and we do not use your location for advertising.

9. Health and prescription data

Prescription photos, doctor and hospital names, and the medicines you order are health-related information. We treat them as confidential:

  • They are shared only with the pharmacy and, where relevant, the clinician handling your consultation.
  • They are never used for marketing and never sold.
  • Access inside Helix is limited to the staff and the pharmacy who need it to serve you.
  • They are retained as described in section 10 so that your prescription history remains available to you and your pharmacist.
Helix supports delivery and dispensing. It does not replace a doctor's advice, and a pharmacist may decline an order that appears unsafe.

10. How long we keep your information

  • Account details — until you delete your account.
  • Orders, prescriptions and delivery records — for as long as Tanzanian pharmacy, tax and health regulations require us to keep them, so your history stays available to you and your pharmacist.
  • Support messages — for as long as needed to resolve your request and for a reasonable period afterwards.
  • Push tokens — until you uninstall the app or disable notifications.
  • Consultation records — for the retention period that applies to health records.

When data is no longer required, we delete it or permanently anonymise it.

11. How we protect your information

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored only as hashes; we can never read your password.
  • Your session token is held in secure storage on your device.
  • Access to our systems is restricted to people who need it to do their job, and is logged.
  • Backups are encrypted and access-controlled.

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever become aware of a breach that affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

12. Your choices and rights

You can, at any time:

  • View and correct your name, phone, email and addresses from Settings in the app.
  • Turn off notifications, location and camera access from your device settings.
  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to delete your account and your personal data, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • Withdraw consent where we process on consent, without affecting processing that already happened.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and ask for data to be corrected if inaccurate.

To exercise any of these, use Help & Support in the app or email support@helix.co.tz. We answer within 30 days and we will not charge you for a reasonable request.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you may raise a complaint with the Office of the Personal Data Protection Commissioner of Tanzania.

13. Children

Helix is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Where a parent or guardian orders medicine for a minor, the data belongs to that parent or guardian and is handled under this policy.

14. Where your data is processed

Our services are operated from Tanzania. Some of the providers listed in section 7 — for notifications, sign-in, video and maps — are global companies that may process data on servers outside Tanzania, including in the United States and the European Union.

Where that happens, we rely on the provider's contractual and technical safeguards, and we only send what that provider needs for its job.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or the law changes. When we make a material change, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use of Helix after an update means you accept the revised policy.
